# TB-01 What Task Buddies is: script

Length: 30 s. Works with the sound off; every line below is on screen.

- The question: Is this another chart I have to police?
- The belief that turns out wrong: A routine app is a checklist with a timer.
- What is true instead: You pick a routine and a buddy stays on screen with your child, one task at a time.

## Question (0.0 to 4.4 s)

A paper-style Morning checklist drawn in brand tokens, two of five rows ticked.

- On screen, 0.5 to 4.2 s: "Not another chart."

## Model (4.4 to 9.4 s)

The ticked rows and the rest fold away, leaving one task, "Get dressed". Hoppy appears beside it, stepped from the real idle sprite sheet.

- On screen, 4.8 to 8.6 s: "One thing at a time."

## Proof (9.4 to 19.9 s)

The real app: the routine picker, then a real Morning run recorded at real speed (Get Dressed, "Done! Feed Hoppy", the chomp, the next task), the last three tasks, the routine-finished screen and the reward revealed. Finished and reward screens are cropped above another product's card.

- On screen, 9.6 to 12.2 s: "Pick a routine."
- On screen, 12.6 to 16.0 s: "The buddy stays with them."

## Turn (19.9 to 24.9 s)

The same buddy carrying on in a Bedtime routine (Put on Pajamas, fed, next task), recorded at real speed.

- On screen, 20.3 to 23.6 s: "Same buddy, another routine."

## Payoff (24.9 to 30.0 s)

The opening checklist returns, fades, and Hoppy celebrating takes its place. End card: logo and taskbuddies.app.

- On screen, 25.6 to 28.1 s: "Not another chart."
