# TB-03 Meet the buddies: script

Length: 20 s. Works with the sound off; every line below is on screen.

- The question: Who is the buddy?
- The belief that turns out wrong: The buddy is one generic mascot.
- What is true instead: There are five buddies, and your child picks one.

## Question (0.0 to 3.0 s)

One ink silhouette where a mascot would be (Hoppy's real idle frames, filled flat).

- On screen, 0.3 to 2.9 s: "Who's the buddy?"

## Model (3.0 to 9.0 s)

The silhouette fills in as Hoppy, then Snapper, Snoozy, Flutty and Buddy arrive one at a time in the app's order, each named and playing its real idle animation from its sprite sheet.


## Proof (9.0 to 13.4 s)

The real buddy selection screen, recorded at real speed: browsing back to Snoozy and tapping "Choose Snoozy".

- On screen, 9.3 to 12.0 s: "They choose."

## Turn (13.4 to 17.2 s)

Snoozy in a running Morning routine, recorded at real speed: "Done! Feed Snoozy", the chomp, the celebration, the next task.

- On screen, 13.6 to 17.0 s: "Then they get going together."

## Payoff (17.2 to 20.0 s)

All five together again; Snoozy steps forward, celebrating. End card: logo and taskbuddies.app.
