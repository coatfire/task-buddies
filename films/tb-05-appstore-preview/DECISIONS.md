# TB-05 App Store preview: decisions

- **A recut, not a new film.** Every shot comes from the TB-02 take (`setup-take.mp4`) or the TB-01 captures (`morning-run.mp4`, `22-routine-finished.png`, `24-reward.png`). Nothing was captured for this film alone.
- **No on-screen text.** Optional in the brief. Over a full-frame app screen, any line would sit on top of real interface. Without it, every word in the preview is the app's own, which also removes any chance of a line reading as aimed at a child.
- **Real speed throughout.** Every clip plays at real speed. The cuts join separate moments; nothing within a clip is sped up.
- **Beat order.** The brief's order (set-up, buddy chosen, running, finished) is kept, although in the take the buddy is chosen before the tasks are edited. A recut reorders moments, and the order follows how a parent would describe it.
- **Hoppy all the way through.** The buddy-chosen shot uses "Choose Hoppy" from the take, rather than the Snoozy selection from TB-03, so the same buddy appears in the running and finished shots.
- **Other product kept out.** The finished and reward screens are the TB-01 crops (top 570 and 621 of 844 px), pinned to the top of the frame. The rest of the frame is the app's own background colour `#FAF3E8`. **Review risk:** Apple asks for app previews to show the app's screen full size. A developer has reported a rejection for a preview with the recording shrunk onto a background. These two shots are not shrunk (full width, real scale), but the lower part of the frame is plain background. If that is a concern, remove the Lovou card from the routine-finished screen in the app and recapture, or end the preview on the running shot.
- **Formats.** 886x1920 covers every iPhone from 6.1" to 6.9"; 1080x1920 covers 5.5" and 4". No 4:5 or 1:1, no carousel stills.
- **Sound.** Silent stereo track, as Apple asks for a stereo track.
